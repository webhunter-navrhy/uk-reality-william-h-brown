SUMMARY

A substantial and beautifully presented four-bedroom detached residence, complemented by a detached annexe, situated in the highly sought-after area of Knox.

DESCRIPTION

A substantial and well-appointed four-bedroom detached home and complemented by a detached annexe. Located in the highly sought-after area of Knox, the property enjoys a superb position close to an excellent range of local amenities, including independent shops, cafés and well-regarded primary schools.

Knox is particularly popular with families and commuters alike, benefitting from public transport links and convenient to Harrogate town centre. The surrounding area offers an abundance of green spaces, including the picturesque Nidd Gorge and scenic riverside walks.

The property itself briefly comprises an entrance hall, living room, family room, kitchen, utility room, bathroom and a ground floor bedroom. To the first floor are three further bedrooms and a family bathroom.

Externally, the rear garden features a combination of paved and gravelled areas with fenced boundaries and two pergolas.

A particular highlight is the detached annexe, offering versatile accommodation ideal for multi-generational living or guest use. It briefly comprises an open-plan kitchen and living area, two bedrooms and a bathroom — representing a rare opportunity in this desirable location.
