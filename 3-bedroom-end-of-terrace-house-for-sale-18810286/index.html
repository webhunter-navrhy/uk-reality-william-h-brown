DESCRIPTION

Needed to be viewed to be appreciated. King Edwards Drive can only be described as a tardis! Upon entering the property via the UPVC door to the front, there is the welcoming hallway with staircase to the first floor and providing, access to the lounge and kitchen. The two reception rooms are incredibly versatile. Currently the lounge is situated to the front of the property and benefits from a large double glazed window, gas fire and double doors to the dining room. The dining room has been used as a playroom and an office and has a double glazed window to the side and serving hatch to the kitchen.The kitchen really is the heart of the home and has been extended to the rear. There is ample storage throughout with space for appliances and a dining table. To the first floor there are three bedrooms al of a generous size and a four piece house bathroom. The loft has been boarded and insulated and subject to planning would make a fabulous bedroom. Externally there is an rear enclosed courtyard with gated access to the ginnel.

This property is close to excellent proximity schools and many amenities on King Edwards Drive. It is certainly one not to be missed! Available CHAIN FREE!
