SUMMARY

[Guide Price £350,000-£360,000] A rare opportunity to purchase a Georgian-era property, located in the heart of South Lowestoft! Complete with four rooms, three of which doubles, an expansive driveway and a dedicated bar outbuilding.

DESCRIPTION

Located in a prime spot in Kirkley, just moments away from a range of amenities including Lowestoft's award-winning beaches, sits this fantastic four-bedroom semi-detached house on Carlton Road. The property itself, built in the 1730s, hosts a range of characteristic features including exposed wooden beams and several functional multi-fuel burners!

Within the property, the ground floor opens up to an array of reception rooms, including separate lounge and dining rooms, a well-equipped kitchen including fittings for multiple white goods, and an additional sunroom to allow full enjoyment of the South-East facing sun!

Upstairs, the property continues to impress with four brilliantly sized bedrooms, all accessed off of the landing. The primary bedroom also features a handy en-suite alongside a luxury walk-in wardrobe.

To the rear, a beautifully secluded garden plays host to a range of amenities, including a fully-insulated dedicated bar outbuilding with power supply, and adequate decking for hosting or relaxing. To the front, an expansive driveway allows for convenient off road parking options, providing space for a family dynamic.
