Description:

An opportunity to acquire a mid-terrace three-bedroom house located within proximity to the Queen Elizabeth Hospital and a variety of local amenities and the town centre. The property has previously been tenanted and could potentially produce £12,000 per annum and is now to being sold with vacant possession. The ground floor accommodation comprises of an entrance hall, lounge/diner, kitchen, the first floor offers three bathrooms. The house would continue to make a sound investment producing a yield of a proximately 10% or a ideal first home.

Accommodation:

Ground Floor: Entrance hall with stairs to first floor, lounge/diner, kitchen.

First Floor: Landing, three bedrooms, bathroom.

Exterior: Front garden laid to lawn with path to front door, enclosed low maintenance rear garden with pedestrian access. En bloc garage.
